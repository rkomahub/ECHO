## Main result

Selective recoupling produces the required whole-register operation
within numerical precision in the ideal longitudinal model.
Global echo removes local phases but leaves unwanted interactions.

Uniform scaling changes gate time cubically without improving ideal
fidelity or changing pulse resources.

## Interpretation limits

Independent instantaneous addressing is assumed.
Coincident isolated-site spectra do not establish that addressing.
Omitted transverse terms, nuclei, leakage and physical drive effects
remain unvalidated in the gate calculation.

No experimental fidelity, optimal physical spacing, or benchmarking
result is claimed.
